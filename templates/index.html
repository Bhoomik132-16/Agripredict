Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does the SCM (Supply Chain) process work?

Once a vendor places an order, our Admin team initiates the SCM protocol. We manage the collection from the farm, quality inspection, and logistics. Contact details are shared only after the initial logistics are verified.

2. What does the 30% commission cover?

The commission is vital for maintaining our ecosystem. It covers end-to-end transportation costs, AI model maintenance, verified buyer filtering, and local hub storage fees.

3. Can I use the Yield Predictor for any crop?

Currently, our AI supports major staples like Rice, Wheat, Maize, Cotton, and Sugarcane across all 28 Indian states. We are constantly expanding our datasets to include more horticultural crops.

Professional Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

Welcome to AgriPredict. We are committed to protecting your agricultural data and personal information. This policy outlines how we handle data for Farmers, Vendors, and Partners.

1. Data Collection

We collect location data (GPS/State), soil nitrogen levels, rainfall estimates, and phone numbers. This data is used solely to refine AI yield models and facilitate B2B connections.

2. Role-Based Visibility

Farmer contact details (Email/Mobile) are strictly hidden from the public. These credentials are only released by the System Admin once a Vendor places a verified order and the SCM process begins.

3. Third-Party Sharing

AgriPredict does NOT sell your data to third-party marketing firms. Data may be shared with logistics partners only to ensure the physical delivery of agricultural produce.

4. Security Protocols

All passwords are encrypted using high-security hashing (Werkzeug). We use industry-standard tokens for session management and database security.
