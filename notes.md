## 🚜 Farmer Features
1.  **AI Crop & Yield Predictor**: Enter land area and soil parameters (N, P, K, pH) to get recommended crops and predicted tonnage.
2.  **Set to List**: A one-click solution to move AI predictions directly into the Marketplace as a "Sell" listing.
3.  **My Active Listings**: Real-time visibility of crops currently listed on the marketplace.
4.  **Sales Tracker (Dashboard)**: Track the status of orders placed on your crops (Placed -> SCM -> Finalized).

## 📈 Platform Ecosystem (Admin)
*   **SCM Protocol**: The System Admin oversees the logistics. Once an order is "SCM Managed", contact details are released to both parties.
*   **Revenue Generation**: The platform records a 30% commission on finalized deals to cover transport and AI overhead.
*   **Support & CRM**: Role-based support ticketing for technical, logistic, or billing issues.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy
*   **Contact Masking**: Farmer contact details are hidden until the SCM process is initiated.
*   **Encryption**: Secure hashing for all user passwords and SSL-simulated encryption for payments.
